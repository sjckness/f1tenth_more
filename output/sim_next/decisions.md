# sim_next decisions (Isaac render fixes, campaign room, LLM planner in sim, office)

Task: `output/sim_next/report.md`. Two repos: `~/isaac_ws` (branch `f1tenth`, the sim) and this one
(`jazzy-campaign`, the stack). Evidence of the Isaac-side parts is in `~/isaac_ws/output/sim_next/`.
Rule followed throughout: when a choice was open, take the option closest to the real car and log it here.

| # | decision | why / evidence |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Cameras render with DLAA (`/rtx/post/aa/op` = 4), forced after app start and again after the scene is built.** Not FXAA, not "AA off" | The Kit 107 real-time renderer accepts only 3 (DLSS) and 4 (DLAA) (`omni.rtx.settings.core` rt_widgets.py; any other value is "Invalid AA Mode"). Measured: the app config `anti_aliasing: 0` read back as 3 at the first frame, and forcing 2 (FXAA) rendered a frame identical to DLSS (mean abs diff 0.28/255). DLAA is DLSS at render scale 1.0: native 640x360. The warning `DLSS increasing input dimensions ... (320, 180)` is gone, mean gradient magnitude of the same frame 2.436 → 2.587 (+6 %), before/after crops in `isaac_ws/output/sim_next/dlss_before_after_crop.png` |
| S2 | **Person: static arms-down pose authored on the character's own skeleton** (`sim/person_pose.py`), not another asset | All /Isaac/People characters ship in the bind T-pose; the clips in /Isaac/People/Animations use another skeleton (0 joints in common with `RL_BoneRoot/...`), so playing `stand_idle_loop` needs omni.anim.people retargeting. A one-frame UsdSkel animation (rest pose, upper arms rotated to hang 4° off vertical) gives a natural standing pose with no run-time cost, keeps label `person` and the capsule collider. Footprint measured on the skinned points (BBoxCache ignores skinning): T-pose 1.872 x 0.420 x 1.788 m → standing **0.577 x 0.420 x 1.788 m** (width x depth x height). The 1.63 x 1.94 m of the old log was the world AABB of the T-pose (2.05 m arm span) rotated by the person's yaw |
| S3 | Person depth stays 0.42 m (target ≤ 0.4) | It is the asset's real depth with the arms in any pose (constant over 0-8° and the T-pose): shoe toes (-0.244) to the coat back (+0.175). Shrinking the mesh would make a person 4 % thinner than the asset's own proportions; the capsule collider (r 0.175) is what the lidar sees |
| S4 | Arm angle 4° (not 8°) | 8° → width 0.651 m (over the 0.6 m target), 4° → 0.577 m, 0° → 0.514 m with hands inside the coat. `tools/person_extent.py` measures any angle |
| S5 | **GUI renders at the camera rate (15 Hz), viewport 640x360, real-time pacing also in GUI mode; `--viewport off` option** | The loop called `world.step(render=True)` on every physics step in GUI mode; a render draws every render product, so both cameras rendered at 60 Hz instead of 15 (4x), and GUI mode had no pacing. RTF (base_room, 60 s): GUI 0.569 before → 1.000 after (capacity unthrottled 1.478; headless 1.536). Sensor rates and resolutions unchanged (900 camera frames in 60 s in every run) |
| S6 | Log noise: `No module named 'lark'` **silenced**; `omni.isaac.*` deprecation warnings **ignored**; `camera rgb not ready` **downgraded to debug for the first 5 renders** | lark: a DEBUG traceback from the bridge's `rosidl_generator_py` (code generator only, not needed for message classes at run time); its logger is set to WARNING. Deprecations: 83 lines, all at extension start-up from compatibility extensions enabled by Isaac's experience file (`isaacsim.exp.base.python.kit`), before any of our code runs; none from our imports. Camera: the annotators return an empty buffer on the first render (pipeline depth); a camera still empty after 5 renders still warns |
| S7 | CPU governor: documented, not changed | Needs sudo (`isaac_ws/output/todo_for_andreas.md` §4). `intel_pstate` "powersave" is dynamic (cores at 4.56 GHz under load) and RTF 1.0 holds with 50 % margin headless |
| S8 | **Potted plant = `/Isaac/Environments/Office/Props/SM_Plant02.usd` at uniform scale 0.55** → 0.516 x 0.554 x 0.855 m, label `potted plant`, static triangle-mesh collider | Candidates measured with `tools/asset_probe.py`: SM_Plant01 1.31 x 1.54 x 1.76, SM_Plant02 0.94 x 1.01 x 1.55, SM_Plant03 0.87 x 0.93 x 0.95, ArchVis Plant_02 0.45 x 0.42 x 0.51, YuccaCane 2.9 m tall, Succulents 0.1 m. Only SM_Plant02 reaches 0.8-1.2 m high and 0.4-0.6 m wide with a uniform scale (0.55; the real campaign plant tracked 0.35-0.45 m wide). The label is the literal COCO name the stack uses (`yolo26s-seg.pt` names; `object_classes.py` go_to enum, no remap anywhere). The plant is static, so an exact triangle collider is allowed: the lidar sees the pot and leaves where they really are (one scan: a hit at -28° and 2.66 m, nothing at -26°) |
| S9 | **Layouts are presets in `config/scenes/base_room.yaml`: `campaign` (default), `obstacle`, `gazebo_6x6`, `e2e_legacy`, all marked ASSUMED** | No document in either repo measures the real room (searched docs/, output/, the first_test_campaign data on campaign-final). Numbers used, relative to the car start: wall 4.5 m ahead of the lidar (M02 R009 front-cone 4.55 m, R011 4.48 m); person 4.2 ahead / 2.2 left (M04 2026-09-24: 4.4-4.8 / 2.2-2.8, fitted into the 6 m room); plant 2.6 ahead / 1.3 **right** (M03 2026-09-28: 2.0-3.2 ahead, 1.1-1.5 *left*, mirrored so that "turn left at the wall", radius 0.92 m at full lock, has free space). `obstacle` adds the Gazebo box_1.0 (1.0 m across, 0.5 deep, 0.8 high) 3.0 m ahead (campaign_scenario.py). `gazebo_6x6` is test_room_6x6 scenario 3 of the jazzy branch (described there as "the real test room", contradicted by run travel of 5.5-6.6 m in M05). `e2e_legacy` keeps the jazzy-port e2e layout |
| S10 | Room size stays 6 x 6 m | Task spec of the Isaac scene; the only measured wall distance (4.5 m ahead) fits it. The campaign runs with longer travel (M02 R008 18 m) were in a bigger space: a bigger room would be a guess too |
| S11 | Ground truth: `/sim/ground_truth/objects`, `vision_msgs/Detection3DArray` in `map_sim`, 1 Hz, reliable + transient_local | One Detection3D per object: `id` (person, plant, obstacle, wall_0..3), `results[0].hypothesis.class_id` (the label), `bbox.center` (pose, yaw) and `bbox.size`. The person's box is the skinned standing mesh in its own frame (x across the shoulders). Static scene, so transient_local lets a late subscriber get it at once. vision_msgs is in the bridge's internal Jazzy libs |
| S12 | Obstacle box `size` is in the box frame, x along the heading at yaw 0: `[0.5, 1.0, 0.8]` | First version had `[1.0, 0.5, 0.8]` and rendered 0.5 m across the path (seen on the snapshot); fixed, lidar now spans ±10° at 2.75 m = 0.97 m |
