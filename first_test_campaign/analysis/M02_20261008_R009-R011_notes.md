# M02 approach the wall, 2026-10-08, R009-R011: why each run stopped

Figures: `M02_20261008_R009-R011_why_it_stopped.png` (map, speed, distances per run),
`M02_20261008_R011_camera_roi.png` (R011 camera frames with the front_distance ROI drawn in yellow),
and `corridor_plot.png` in every 2026-10-08 run folder.

Plan in all three runs (LLM intent `{"mode":"straight","guard":"wall"}`, no thresh): one straight
move at 0.4 m/s, ended by `front_clearance < 2.0 m` (debounce 3 ticks). The 2.0 m is the
translator default `WALL_THRESH_DEFAULT_M`; the M02 criterion is 0.5 m. The guard reads the camera
`/perception/front_distance`, which is meant to exclude detected objects.

| run | what happened | why it stopped |
|-----|---------------|----------------|
| R009 | person walked in ~2 m ahead before the start | the guard fired on the person, with the person ~1.5 m from the car centre (about 1.1 m gap). The wall was at least 3.6 m away: before the person walked in, the LiDAR front-cone minimum was 4.55 m and the car then drove 0.94 m. The person leaked into front_distance. It held while the person was ~2 m away and fired as the car closed to ~1.5 m. |
| R010 | plant in place 1.8 m ahead from the start | it worked. The car swerved left around the plant (tight pass) and the guard fired on the wall, 1.7 m ahead by LiDAR (about 0.3 m of filter and debounce lag on the 2.0 m threshold). |
| R011 | plant enters the LiDAR and camera only 4-6 s into the run, ~1.4 m ahead | the MPC braked and steered to full left lock (0.318 of 0.319 rad) but could not clear the plant. It then asked for 0.087-0.098 m/s for 32 s, and the car never moved: the LiDAR range to the wall stayed at 4.48-4.49 m. In every run the car started moving only once the command reached 0.12-0.14 m/s. The one reverse request (-0.089 m/s) was clamped to 0 (`max_reverse_speed_mps: 0`). The wall guard was correctly silent because the wall was 4.5 m away. The mission ended (`stop_reason stop_condition:front_clearance`) only when the operator walked up to the plant, at ~1.3 m. The 80 s move timeout would otherwise have aborted it. |

## What is proven and what is not

- Proven from logs: which condition ended each move (behaviour log plus MoveOutcome), the wall and
  object distances (LiDAR, camera tracks), and the stall (commands vs LiDAR).
- Not observable: the value of front_distance itself. The test-campaign folder and the archive bag
  record neither front_distance nor depth. So the pixel-level reason the person leaked in R009
  cannot be read back. Candidates, none confirmed:
  - at ~1.2 m the 180x70 px ROI sees only the person's shins (about 7-41 cm high), where the
    segmentation mask is roughly drawn;
  - the 5th-50th percentile band is the near half of the ROI, so leaked near pixels pull the mean
    hard;
  - the latest YOLO mask is applied to the current depth frame without time alignment.
